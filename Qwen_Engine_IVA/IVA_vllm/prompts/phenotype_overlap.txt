TASK:
For each condition, decide whether it covers any part of the patient's phenotype. A condition covers a patient finding when that finding, or a more specific form of it, is the condition's name or one of its listed clinical features — even if it is not the condition's main feature. When a condition has no listed features, judge it by what its name states. The MedGen entry was found by name search: if its title is clearly a different disease from the condition (a different mechanism, inheritance or clotting factor, not merely a synonym or numbered subtype), ignore its features and judge that condition by its name.

Write one line per condition, in this exact form:
- <condition>: MATCH | NO MATCH — <at most 15 words: the matching feature, or why none>

Then ONE verdict line, in exactly this form:
CLUSTER_PHENOTYPE: YES | PARTIAL | INCIDENTAL

YES        — at least one matched condition covers the patient's phenotype as a whole.
PARTIAL    — matched conditions cover at least one distinct part of the patient's phenotype, but not all of it.
INCIDENTAL — no condition in the list matches any part of the patient's phenotype.

One MATCH is enough for YES or PARTIAL, however many other conditions do not match.
