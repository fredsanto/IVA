Weight the retrieved evidence as follows:

- "NO DISEASE LINK" → reason to discard (gene unrelated to phenotype).

A real patient's phenotype often lists findings from more than one distinct
cluster (e.g. a neurodevelopmental cluster — epilepsy, intellectual disability,
autism — AND a separate cluster — an organ malformation, a cancer, a skeletal
finding). "NO DISEASE LINK" / "gene unrelated to phenotype" only applies when the
evidence affirmatively links this gene to NONE of the patient's clusters. If the
evidence links the gene to AT LEAST ONE distinct cluster, that is a reason to KEEP
— even if the evidence is silent on the patient's other cluster(s). Being silent
on a different cluster is not the same as ruling the gene out; only discard on
phenotype grounds when the mismatch is across every cluster, not just one.
