CAUSATIVE THRESHOLD RULE (mandatory, applies uniformly to EVERY MOI layer —
De Novo, Dominant-Inherited, Recessive, X-Linked — no layer gets
a different bar): a variant is CAUSATIVE, and MUST be listed in section 2, if
its own layer/base block's total ACMG point value is >= 6 (Tavtigian et al.
2020: 6-9 = Likely Pathogenic, >=10 = Pathogenic). This is a per-variant
threshold check, not a competition — every qualifying variant across every
layer goes in section 2, even if several different variants/genes each clear
the bar and even if one of them scores higher than the others. Do NOT pick
only the single highest-scoring variant and drop the rest to reasoning-only
mention or omit them entirely once they clear >=6 points. The only reasons a
>=6-point variant may be excluded from section 2 are the ones already listed
elsewhere in these rules (TRANS STATUS / CIS collapse, SINGLE-HIT RECESSIVE
CHECK, COMPOUND-HET JOINT THRESHOLD EXCEPTION below, a stated factual
inconsistency in its own block) — never "a stronger finding already explains
the phenotype" or "already covered by another gene" as a reason alone to drop
a second independently-qualifying variant. A variant scoring below 6 points
(VUS or below) is never causative regardless of layer, and falls to section 4
(if its total is >= 4 and < 6) or is omitted per the SCOPE RULE below.

COMPOUND-HET JOINT THRESHOLD EXCEPTION (mandatory, applies only to a
RECESSIVE layer block of the "(compound heterozygous / biallelic)" PAIR kind
— never to a "(confirmed homozygous, biallelic)" single-variant block, which
is unaffected by this rule): a compound-het pair is causative as a PAIR, not
as two independent single-variant threshold checks. The block already states
its own backend-computed "**Joint compound-het classification:**" line —
copy that verdict verbatim rather than re-deriving it. If it says CAUSATIVE,
list both variants in section 2 as co-causative per STEP 2 below. If it says
COMPOUND VUS, do NOT list either variant in section 2 as Pathogenic/Likely
Pathogenic — even though one partner's own individual total may be >= 6 —
because a single strong allele in a recessive gene does not by itself
establish a biallelic diagnosis; the weak/VUS partner means the second hit
required for a recessive mechanism is not confirmed. In that case, mention
the pair only under section 4 (Notable VUS) if either variant's own total is
>= 4 and < 6 points, per the normal VUS rule.
