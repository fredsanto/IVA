ACMG criteria guidance (point value in brackets, per Tavtigian et al. 2020 Bayesian point system):
- PVS1 [VeryStrong, +8]: Loss-of-function variant in a gene where LoF is a known disease mechanism (use AutoPVS1 output when available). Only apply if Phenotype fit above is "match" — a LoF mechanism alone does not justify PVS1 if the gene is not linked to the patient's phenotype (Phenotype fit "mismatch" or "uncertain"). If phenotype fit is not a match, do not apply PVS1 regardless of AutoPVS1 strength; note the mechanism as unconfirmed disease relevance instead.
- PS1 [Strong, +4]: Same amino acid change as a previously established pathogenic variant.
- PS3 [Strong, +4]: Well-established in vitro or in vivo functional studies supporting a damaging effect. When multiple studies conflict, weight the most recent publication (highest year in the [PMID:XXXXXXXX, YEAR] citation) more heavily and note the disagreement rather than silently picking one.
- PS4 [Strong, +4]: The prevalence of the variant in affected individuals is significantly increased over its prevalence in controls (case-control literature evidence).
- PM1 [Moderate, +2]: Located in a mutational hot spot and/or critical and well-established functional domain.
- PM2 [Moderate, +2]: Absent from controls in population databases (gnomAD, ExAC) or at very low frequency. Check this independently of de novo/PS2 status — always report the Frequency field.
- PM5 [Moderate, +2]: Novel missense change at an amino acid residue where a different missense pathogenic variant has been documented.
- PP3 [Supporting, +1]: Multiple lines of computational evidence support a deleterious effect. Only apply this criterion if the evidence above contains actual named in-silico tool scores (e.g. REVEL, CADD, SIFT, PolyPhen-2, AlphaMissense, SpliceAI). If no in-silico score is present in the evidence, do NOT apply PP3 — a vague "computational evidence supports pathogenicity" without a named tool and score is not sufficient grounding for this criterion.
  When scores ARE present, evaluate each one against its standard pathogenicity threshold below (do not just report the number — judge whether it crosses the deleterious threshold):
    - REVEL: ≥0.7 supports deleterious; ≤0.3 supports benign; in between is inconclusive.
    - CADD (Phred-scaled): ≥20 supports deleterious (top ~1% most damaging); below is weaker/inconclusive.
    - SIFT: <0.05 supports deleterious ("damaging"); ≥0.05 supports tolerated/benign.
    - PolyPhen-2: >0.85 supports deleterious ("probably damaging"); 0.15–0.85 possibly damaging/inconclusive; <0.15 supports benign.
    - AlphaMissense: >0.564 supports deleterious ("likely pathogenic"); <0.34 supports benign ("likely benign"); in between is ambiguous.
    - SpliceAI (delta score): ≥0.5 supports a splice-altering effect; <0.2 does not.
  Apply PP3 only when the reported scores actually cross their deleterious threshold — cite each tool's name and score value (e.g. "REVEL=0.82, CADD=28.3") alongside the judgment. If a score falls in the inconclusive/benign range, say so explicitly and do not count it toward PP3.
- PP4 [Supporting, +1]: Patient's phenotype or family history is highly specific for a disease with a single known genetic etiology. Only apply PP4 if the evidence indicates this disease/phenotype is caused by variants in ONE gene (not genetically heterogeneous — i.e. not a condition where multiple different genes are each independently known to cause the same phenotype). If the evidence shows or does not rule out genetic heterogeneity for this phenotype, do NOT apply PP4 — state the heterogeneity explicitly instead. Regardless of how phenotype-specific the match is, the point value used for PP4 must always stay below 5 (Supporting [+1] by default; Moderate [+2] at most if evidence strongly justifies it — never Strong [+4] or higher).
- BA1 [Standalone Benign, -8]: Allele frequency > 5% in population databases.
- BS1 [Strong Benign, -4]: Allele frequency greater than expected for disorder.
- BS4 [Strong Benign, -4]: Lack of segregation with disease in affected family members. Do not apply this to outweigh a strong LoF/PVS1 finding without explicitly reconciling the conflict in the Comment.
- BP1 [Supporting, -1]: Missense variant in a gene for which primarily truncating variants are known to cause disease.
- BP7 [Supporting, -1]: A synonymous variant for which no splicing disruption is predicted.
